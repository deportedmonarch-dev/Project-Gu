## Confirmed decisions and recommended defaults

| Status | Decision and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** | Use a persisted, latched minimum-to-maximum/capacity recovery cycle for each applicable population. Its input is anonymous units plus eligible living residents, with legitimate removals deducted once. Use durable origin/transfer evidence for abstract replenishment, migration and recolonization, formation, and environmental, human or animal influences. Regional populations and unloaded recovery are scheduled Alpha 6 deliverables with bounded sparse work and zero adjacent map generation. |
| **Confirmed** | Species history and an individual's origin are separate. Natural Gu can replenish in aggregate. True reproduction requires a compatible explicitly opted-in artificial species and a qualified environment. Legitimate formation, migration and introductions can colonize a previously absent locality. An initialized-empty marker blocks free initialization replay. Wild nourishment remains resource-neutral in both aggregate and native representations. Preserve 5B possession feeding and off-map policy. |
| **Confirmed** | Each independently established spring owns separate populations. Ordinary spring profiles apply hard geography/environment eligibility before weights. Gu Yue's cultivated Moon Orchid ecosystem is a specialized authored or deliberate setting. Preserve established 5B ecosystems, locations, counts, flora and history without profile rerolls. Stable site, level and spring ownership survive map lifecycle and prevent duplicate seeding/support. |
| **Confirmed** | Black Boar Gu, White Boar Gu and Stone Skin Gu are the common roster. Liquor Worm remains rare, with the existing spring beer lure preserved. Future configurable alcohol attraction targets existing eligible stock only. |
| **Initial balance targets** | RimWorld's pinned `GenDate` uses 60,000 ticks per day and 60 days per year. Target 3–9 days per ordinary replacement under favorable conditions and approximately 1–2 days for an eligible one-time artificial founding pair. Species intervals, thresholds, climate effects, founding recipes and inactive-work bounds are tuning. Multi-year ordinary replenishment is outside the baseline. |
| **Recommended defaults, pending confirmation** | Bounded regional recovery reads last reliable local evidence, regional suitability, available sources and elapsed time. Healthy unloaded habitats can recover. Destroyed habitats need evidence of restoration or an authorized environmental model. Accelerated artificial founding requires independent habitat identity and valid species provenance. Replanting, temporary invalidation, relocation, reload and depletion preserve the entitlement's history. Introduced individuals retain their IDs as existing stock and confer no extra free Gu. |
| **Deferred implementations** | Expanded active and passive Liquor Worm attraction, NPC and animal provisioning, milk-spout transport, and production systems for blessed lands and immortal economies. Carry logical spring ID, sole ownership and founding history in the relocation contract. Author additional Earth/Water/stone/hibernating or Bone/Blood content when its mechanics are approved. |

## Initial roster and content decisions

The common roster and rarity are confirmed. Recommend one Black Boar Gu habitat, one ordinary discovery and one authorized recovery source for the first playable slice. Use a matched unsuitable control. The chosen encounter should be legible from ordinary exploration and preserve scarcity through removal, reload and disruption. The reviewed gameplay candidate has no species Def for any of the three selected Gu. The table describes proof responsibilities and content still needing approval.

| Species | Delivery role | Unresolved species content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Black Boar Gu** | First playable surface proof of habitat eligibility, discovery, registry materialization, conservation and threshold recovery. | Choose a geographic fit, observable habitat and unsuitable control, discovery cue, manifestation and capture affordance, diet or explicit unimplemented status, historical origin, gameplay ability/refinement plan and balance. A native boar body is one possible presentation choice. |
| **White Boar Gu** | Add after Black Boar Gu passes focused native verification. Aim for additional Def data and species-specific gameplay/presentation components only. Review any proposed shared adapter extension at the boundary it actually needs. ## Release and authorization gates

- Authorize the Black Boar Gu content seam, first habitat signal, readable discovery and scarcity. Keep Slice 0 on shared registry transactions. Approve species-specific native behavior before choosing the first live manifestation.
- Accept the Alpha 5B baseline and its upgrade scope. Recheck any subsequent accepted commit against the table above.
- Treat the two Alpha 5A failures as regression evidence. Require a deterministic isolated fixture with explicit preconditions and identity-scoped outcomes before declaring the flakiness closed. Keep missing-bait UI, terminal outcomes, and exactly-once notifications as requirements.
- Preserve all committed IDs and pause inactive recovery in the first surface slice. Add bounded unloaded and regional recovery in Slice 2. Confirm the regional evidence policy and later return significance rules before those slices, and approve the first artificial profile before farming content is authored.
- Gate any new wild Pawn on subsistence/curiosity, aging and stages, legacy load, reproduction, taming and training tests. Preserve ordinary animal controls and legitimate spending through 5B feeding, harvest and deliberate lure jobs.
- Authorize only the registry seam and the selected playable slice initially. Habitat policy and information disclosure must explain the observed encounter through the environment. Randomness chooses among eligible opportunities with finite stock and real constraints.
