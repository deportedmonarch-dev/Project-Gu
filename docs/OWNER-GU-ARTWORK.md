Future Bronze Skin and Iron Skin production paths are `Textures/ProjectGU/Gu/BronzeSkinGu/Wild_*.png` and `Textures/ProjectGU/Gu/IronSkinGu/Wild_*.png`.

The wild pawn uses native `Graphic_Multi` with unmodified color and draw sizes of `0.70` for both Boars and `0.65` for Stone Skin. The Stone Skin corpse retains the exact prior temporary art and tint at `ProjectGU/Gu/StoneSkinGu/Dessicated`. The stock Black Boar corpse and the temporary White Boar corpse remain in use. Dormant, icon and defensive-effect references retain their existing assets. Owner replacements for those separate forms were outside this delivery.

All five sets have shallower side silhouettes that sit lower on the canvas. The offset is most pronounced for White Boar and the three Skin sets. Black Boar has a taller front and rear silhouette than either side. The visual check should assess apparent scale and vertical movement without altering the source files.

## Verification and playtest

On RimWorld 1.7.5021 rev1365 and .NET 10, structural and managed checks passed. Clean and upgrade installs matched all 12 production hashes and excluded the archives. The disposable `RegionalEcology` campaign `482cfe300c6041b89155f5b0bdcc7785` passed registered spawn and exact-ID fresh-process reload for all three species. Native `Graphic_Multi` resolved all 12 directional materials on both runs. The explicit west sprites used the unflipped mesh; Stone Skin’s three-direction corpse set reused east with the flipped mesh.

Use a throwaway colony with Development Mode enabled. Use **Project GU → Spawn registered wild Gu…** to spawn each implemented species on open ground. Watch all four facings at normal zoom and compare size, alignment and readability on both light and dark ground. Save while the three Gu are still wild, restart RimWorld and reload the throwaway save. Check the sprites and the game log again before recording rendered owner acceptance.
