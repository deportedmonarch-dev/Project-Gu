# Project GU vision

Project GU brings a living cultivation society inspired by *Reverend Insanity* to RimWorld 1.7. Scarcity, cultivation, clan relationships, and competing ambitions should create choices and consequences. The project owner approves gameplay scope and acceptance.

## Approved design principles

- **Gu are living individuals.** Species may move in the wild, interact, feed, become dormant, be captured or refined, enter apertures, and participate in abilities. Species drive which behaviors apply.
- **Identity survives representation and custody changes.** Support Pawn-like active Gu, selectable dormant Things, physical inventory, and aperture storage. Physical possession and refinement ownership can differ. Keep one persistent identity and one authoritative custody state. Select storage and lifecycle mechanisms using verified RimWorld 1.7 evidence.
- **Containment scales.** A cultivator can hold many Gu, and an ability can involve one or many existing Gu. Containment and ability use should avoid the cost of simulating an active Pawn per participant.
- **Ecology supports multiple origins.** Gu may arise through reproduction, natural refinement, regional ecology, biological relationships, or special events. Habitats can improve suitability without being mandatory for every species. Track origins and enforce conservation and applicable uniqueness across these pathways.
- **Underground infrastructure comes first.** Start with a spirit spring cavern and build reusable pocket-map infrastructure for mines, inheritances, shelters, and deep Earth Abyss-style sites.
- **Protect established gameplay and saves.** The above-ground spring is a validated prototype. Keep its tested ecology, production, awakening, and migration behavior as regression baselines.

## Design space

A cultivation interface, feeding jobs and facilities, region-scale ecology, exceptional Gu, formations, autonomous cultivator societies, clan politics, and resource markets are future directions. Their rules, balance, and delivery scope need design and acceptance decisions. [RFC-0002](RFC-0002-GU-INDIVIDUALS-AND-CUSTODY.md) proposes one way to establish individual Gu. The [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) tracks implementation and evidence.
