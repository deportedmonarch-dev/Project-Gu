# Future beast ecology

Beasts can acquire Gu naturally or through deliberate engineering. A beast's relationship with its Gu can be parasitic or mutualistic. Both relationships handle Gu feeding abstractly, with no separate player-managed diet.

## Parasitism

- Rare, harmful relationships drain blood, soul, sanity, lifespan or other resources from the host.
- Without mitigation, affected beasts generally burn out within about five in-game days. They may behave like manhunters and cannot be tamed.
- Parasitic hosts can appear naturally or during hostile ecological events such as wolf tides and tribulations.
- Hosts generally range from Ordinary Beasts through Hundred Beast Kings.

## Mutualism

- More common and sustainable. The beast provides shelter, nourishment and natural essence. The Gu grants instinctive abilities.
- Hosts retain their ordinary diets and feeding behavior. Mutualism alone does not cause manhunter behavior.
- Mutualism occurs in every beast tier and commonly characterizes stronger beasts and pack leaders.

## Progression and player interaction

Ordinary Beast → Hundred Beast King → Thousand Beast King → Myriad Beast King → Beast Emperor.

Exceptional mutated beasts and mutated emperors also exist. Higher-tier beasts command packs. Future gameplay includes natural Gu acquisition, deliberate beast engineering, ecological events and deployment of engineered beasts for defense and offense.
