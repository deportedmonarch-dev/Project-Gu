# Alpha 6 Slice 2 · Regional and unloaded ecology

## Implementation design

The accepted surface owner already holds cohorts by native layer, tile, slot zero and species. Keep those keys, the saved rows and the materialization source directory. Regional donors are additional rows in the same owner. Spring cohorts and existing native projections keep their current owners. Anonymous stock and exact eligible registry IDs jointly determine local occupancy. Ambiguous registry bindings suspend credits. A transient scope index supports resident and receipt lookups.

Partition the native surface sphere using fixed Cartesian buckets of normalized tile-center coordinates, with bucket width one sixth of the unit sphere's radius. Layer ID and the versioned bucket coordinates identify a region. A donor is keyed by region and species. A request requires a suitable adjacent native tile that shares the bucket. The donor saves a suitable tile as its geography witness. XML rules score biome, hilliness and local exposed rock independently of species identity. Both Boars use the forest profile. Flat forest qualifies for Stone Skin only with measured exposed rock, and its regional donor requires native hills. Other layers need their own evidence and geography rules.

Realize a local row at a qualifying settlement observation, including its zero founding result. Retain the accepted 0–3 seeded founding roll. Realize a donor when an eligible migration opportunity needs one. A seed derived from the world, region and species determines its initial stock of 1–3 adults. The same donor supplies later requests and can replenish one unit per interval after its own threshold latches. Its capacity is four.

Save one consumed tick and the remaining interval on each row. The same ledger settles loaded observations, unload boundaries and scheduled unloaded work. A transient ordered queue holds one due entry per realized row. Service a limited number every 250 ticks and settle on qualifying map touch. Supported elapsed time beyond 30 overdue days is discarded and recorded. Ordinary scheduled unloaded intervals run on the same 3–9 day cadence. A load rebuilds scheduling indexes from saved rows and clocks. Threshold one latches a local cycle to capacity four.

Loaded observations measure the existing species prerequisites. Map retirement records final reliable evidence. A healthy unloaded locality uses that evidence while native geography still agrees. A measured absence, destroyed settlement, ambiguous occupancy or missing prerequisite suspends recovery and consumes the unsupported time without paying down the interval. Restoration establishes a new eligible observation. Reload and map generation retain the founding decision and the outstanding interval.

Species policy orders ordinary sources. Established local rows prefer abstract natural replenishment, and an absent row can use finite regional migration when its habitat qualifies. Declared natural formation requires a separate configured source rule and its local environmental evidence. Regional rows replenish through the ordinary natural clock. Migration validates both revisions, stock, occupancy and habitat before a single callback-free debit and credit. Store source receipts with the opportunity and, for migration, both owners and revisions. Introduction and escape accept an existing, exact active wild registry binding after custody release, preserve its original creation receipt and add one habitat membership. Habitat observation by itself moves no units.

The Development Mode action **Log regional and local Gu ecology** reports cohort balances, residents, founding evidence, cursor and due times, blocked reasons, source receipts and scheduler counters. The existing encounter and search routes retain their own knowledge and placement rules.

## Limits

- Regional transfers stay within one native surface bucket and require an adjacent compatible tile. Bucket boundaries can separate otherwise compatible neighboring tiles. Region founding and replenishment use anonymous stock.
- Flat-forest rock evidence is measured from a live map. Unloaded recovery uses the last reliable observation and current native geography. Declared formation currently requires a loaded map. The engine's ordinary settlement and map-retention rules still govern when the player can revisit an unloaded site.
- Introduction and escape expose validated entry points for existing active Gu. The fixture exercises admission with explicit test escape evidence. Native corpse escape and individual preservation also have their own lifecycle regression coverage.

Keep at least one wild resident and capture another. Check that the cohort totals and preserved IDs agree in the Development Mode log.

On a disposable run that can unload the native map without destroying its settlement, let the deadline pass and revisit. Check that the same cohort continues through capacity four, including any living world-site resident. Repeat across a quit to desktop.
3. On flat forest, check Stone Skin with its exposed natural rock gone. Verify recruitment is blocked while the Boars remain eligible. Use the Development Mode log to distinguish restored eligibility, natural recruitment and actual migration. Compare each migration's donor revision with the credited row.
4. Recheck sustained Stone Skin, Black and White Boar cultivation, and an existing spring's search or lure and passage hauling. Keep original historical saves intact.
