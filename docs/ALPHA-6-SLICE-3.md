The rev1365 assembly confirms map teardown despawns pawns before assigning native holders or calling `WorldPawns.PassToWorld` with its ordinary admission mode. `RemoveAndDiscardPawnViaGC` removes the Pawn from the world list before discard. `Pawn.Discard` can clear relations and notify quests, tales and logs. Existing native relationships, ownership or quest evidence therefore block ordinary retirement. Project GU captures location and custody before map teardown and resolves queued evidence after the native call.

Live map return uses reversible `DeSpawnOrDeselect` staging. Only a detached, unheld, living and exact projection may commit. `PassToWorld(..., Discard)` is irreversible native cleanup and runs after the callback-free registry and cohort commit. A death or actual holder transfer during staging takes precedence. Native cleanup failure after commit leaves a suppressed stale projection and the credited receipt. Both `GameDataSaveLoader.SaveGame` and `SafeSaver.Save` for the `savegame` root check the custody guard before any writer runs. The native deep writer catches component serialization exceptions, so an exception inside `ExposeData` cannot provide this guarantee by itself.

## 3A · Encounter retention and return

- Ordinary surface Gu receive one in-game day out of sight before they can return. A nearby awake humanlike with line of sight to a revealed cell, a selected encounter, a completed search result, pursuit or a job or reservation targeting the Pawn renews short protection. Searching an area temporarily protects a nearby revealed result. These checks do not store observer knowledge, reveal fog or move the camera. The existing deliberate search keeps its result presentation. Inspection has no permanent effect.
- Capture, inventory transfer, refinement, faction changes, release and escape extend historical significance to 60 in-game days. Both intervals are configurable in Project GU settings. The species Def's `wildIdentityRetentionDays` can extend the quiet interval or preserve identity indefinitely with `-1`. Unique Gu, immortal ranks and species-defined preservation ranks always keep identity. Active holders, refinement and pending native interactions, unresolved evidence, quest reservations, relationship evidence and persistent wounds block return. Named tracking and investigation references can renew an expiry independently of history.
- Return requires a recorded surface membership and an eligible live habitat under the current map binding. It credits one adult against the same population that counted the resident. A full or inconsistent destination preserves the exact individual. Return changes neither the founding result nor the recovery clock, and delays new manifestation at that species row for one day. A subsequent manifestation debits one adult and uses a new ID.
- The aggregate tombstone retains the original ID, prior custody revision, destination and credit revision. The habitat seals the credited balance and matches its return digest against registry receipts. Compaction removes old narrative state while retaining creation and return evidence. Exact replay performs no credit and cannot authorize a retired body.
- A pristine, unowned body can be restored to the map if staged detachment fails its revalidation. If the map cannot receive it, the coordinator asks native WorldPawns to retain that exact body. A native detachment exception blocks another return attempt for the same binding. A successful return clears the registry's transient native Pawn and host references.

## 3B · Site continuity

- Exact recovery requires the recorded native parent, tile and world layer. An independent underground level also requires its saved level ID. A legacy pocket can use the native source parent recorded at teardown. A regenerated map under the same parent can accept a retained Gu without another cohort debit. A replacement parent can observe the existing anonymous habitat, including its founding result, stock, receipts and clock, while the exact Gu stays at its recorded site.
- The registry captures native locations and holders before `Game.DeinitAndRemoveMap` and reconciles the observed custody after that call. An interrupted teardown leaves an intact map loaded. A holder that moves to the world keeps the same inventory or aperture Gu. The native world-Pawn retirement path is used only for a qualifying unowned body and must finish before the record moves to world-site custody. Injured or otherwise protected native world Pawns keep their exact body and binding.
- Native settlement abandonment and destruction are recorded separately. Habitat founding remains a tile-and-layer decision. Exact records keep their original parent evidence after that parent is removed. Off-map return needs a safe route back to a live habitat under the current transaction contract.
- Compaction checks the return receipt against the sealed cohort evidence. A failed evidence check preserves the detailed tombstone. Once compacted, the record retains the original ID, creation debit, return credit and replay key.

## 3C · Physical residents and fog

- Each species can manifest up to two ordinary physical Gu per locality. The shared setting defaults to six and accepts 1–32. The ambient pass rotates its species order and attempts at most one manifestation per species each 2,500 ticks. It counts existing authorized surface bodies toward the limits. An existing physical Gu keeps its normal retention policy if a limit is reduced. A protected resident can recover its exact identity even when the ambient limit is full.
- Every new ambient body spends one mature unit. The native spawn path rechecks habitat, cell occupancy, suitability, stock or custody and the relevant limit before publication. The conservation capacity of four per species includes anonymous juveniles, anonymous adults and living habitat residents including record-only ones.
- Wild surface placement can use fogged, standable natural terrain that passes the existing outdoor and habitat rules. Player searches still check visibility and reachability. Fresh-process verification preserved hidden Pawns, exact IDs, fog, cohort balances, and a return receipt. Capture and other dormant Thing placement use revealed cells.
- Reconciliation uses a bounded work queue for living records. The registry keeps one exact tombstone per return as permanent replay proof and drops its old projection, lease and narrative data. The archive footprint increases with the number of distinct audited returns.
